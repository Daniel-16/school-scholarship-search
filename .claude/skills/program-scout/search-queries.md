## Scholarship queries

Aggregator feeds respond to how the posts are titled, not to field names:

- "fully funded masters scholarship"
- "fully funded PhD scholarship"
- "scholarship for Nigerian students"
- "scholarship for African students"
- "developing countries scholarship masters"
- "<country> government scholarship"
- "computer science scholarship masters"

Named schemes worth a direct search each cycle:

| Scheme | Level | Note |
|---|---|---|
| DAAD EPOS | MSc, PhD | Development-related, Germany, Nigeria is a priority country |
| Chevening | MSc | UK, one year taught, leadership-scored |
| Commonwealth Scholarships | MSc, PhD | UK, development-focused |
| Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters | MSc | Europe, multi-country, fully funded |
| Mastercard Foundation Scholars | MSc | Several partner universities |
| Swedish Institute (SISGP) | MSc | Sweden, fully funded |
| Holland Scholarship / Orange Knowledge | MSc | Netherlands |
| MEXT | MSc, PhD | Japan, via embassy or university |
| Fulbright Foreign Student | MSc, PhD | US, via the embassy in Abuja |
| Gates Cambridge / Clarendon / Rhodes | MSc, PhD | Highly competitive |
| Knight-Hennessy | MSc, PhD | Stanford, any discipline |
| Aga Khan Foundation ISP | MSc, PhD | Needs-based, partial |
| Islamic Development Bank | MSc, PhD | Member countries including Nigeria |

Verify every one of these on the funder's own site before use — deadlines and
eligibility move annually, and the aggregators lag by a cycle.

## Country priorities

`[CONFIRM]` with the user. Reasonable defaults given cost and eligibility:

1. **Germany** — no tuition at public universities, strong CS/robotics,
   English-taught master's, DAAD. Blocked account is the hurdle.
2. **Netherlands, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Norway** — English-taught,
   strong research, Nordic tuition applies to non-EU students except Norway.
3. **UK** — large funded-PhD market, one-year master's, high cost unfunded.
4. **Canada, Australia** — good post-study work, expensive without funding.
5. **US** — best funded PhDs in CS, weakest fit for direct entry on this
   profile without a master's first.

## Exclusions

Never search for, and drop if returned:

- undergraduate, bachelor's, foundation, diploma, pre-master's
- short courses, summer schools, language courses
- programmes taught in a language other than English
- schemes that exclude Nigerian nationals
- unaccredited institutions
